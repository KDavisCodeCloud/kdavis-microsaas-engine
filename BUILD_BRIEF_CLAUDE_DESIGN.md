## 1. VISUAL PERSONALITY STATEMENT

Small Portfolio Hub is the **trustworthy neighbor**, not the enterprise landlord. Where Buildium feels like a property management company's back office and AppFolio reads like a VC-funded scale machine, Small Portfolio Hub presents as the tool built *by someone who gets it* — calm confidence, plain language, and zero surprises on your invoice.

The visual language should communicate:

- **Clarity over complexity** — every screen, button, and pricing line should feel like it was written by someone who hates fine print
- **Warmth without informality** — this is someone's livelihood; the tone is assured and supportive, never startup-bro casual
- **Competence you can touch** — structured layouts, deliberate whitespace, and a typographic hierarchy that earns trust before a single word is read

**Benchmark positioning:** Warmer than Buildium (too corporate-blue, PM-company energy), more structured than a generic landlord blog, less cold than AppFolio's enterprise finish. Closest visual ancestor is Rentec Direct's no-nonsense directness — but elevated with better typography and a modern layout grid.

## 2. PALETTE APPLICATION

> **Hard rule:** No new brand colors are invented here. All values below are derived strictly from the provided industry palette.

### Provided Palette
| Role | Hex | Name |
|---|---|---|
| Primary Accent | `#2563eb` | Trust Blue |
| Secondary Accent | `#f97316` | Action Orange |

### Application Architecture

#### Trust Blue `#2563eb` — The Anchor
Trust Blue carries the product's credibility. It is used for:
- **Primary navigation bar** background (reversed — white wordmark on blue)
- **Primary CTA buttons** (solid fill, white label: "Start Free Trial", "Get My Price")
- **Active state indicators** — sidebar nav items, tab underlines, selected form fields (border highlight)
- **Pricing tier header** for the flagship/recommended plan card
- **Section headers** on the landing page (H2 level, not H1 — H1 stays near-black for authority)
- **Icon fills** for feature checkmarks and trust badges
- **Footer background** (full-width, reversed white type)

Trust Blue must **never** appear at full saturation as a large background fill outside nav/footer. Applied to a hero section, it becomes aggressive and enterprise-y — exactly the Buildium energy we are differentiating from.

#### Action Orange `#f97316` — The Activator
Action Orange is the conversion engine of the palette. It is sparse, purposeful, and always tied to user momentum. Used for:
- **Urgency/value callouts** — "No hidden fees. Ever." badge, pricing transparency ribbon
- **Secondary CTA buttons** — "See How It Works" (ghost variant with orange border + orange label)
- **Highlight text** within body copy — inline callout for a key differentiator sentence per section (one instance max)
- **Progress indicators** in the onboarding wizard — active step dot/fill
- **"Most Popular" plan badge** on pricing cards
- **Hover state** on primary blue CTA buttons (blue → orange transition communicates responsiveness)
- **Notification dot** on the live chat widget

Action Orange must **never** appear as a background fill for an entire section or as a large hero color. It earns its power through scarcity.

#### Supporting Neutrals (derived, not invented)
These are compositional necessities, built from the palette's implied mood (professional/warm):

| Role | Value | Derivation Logic |
|---|---|---|
| Page background | `#f8fafc` | Near-white, slight cool tint — lets blue breathe without glare |
| Card surface | `#ffffff` | Pure white for data-dense UI components |
| Body text | `#1e293b` | Near-black slate — warmer than pure #000, less harsh |
| Secondary text | `#64748b` | Muted slate — subheadings, metadata, placeholder text |
| Border/divider | `#e2e8f0` | Hairline gray — structures without competing |
| Success state | `#16a34a` | Standard accessible green — rent received, lease signed confirmations |

> Neutrals are compositional infrastructure. The two brand colors remain the only expressive palette voices.

## 3. TYPOGRAPHY

> **Hard rule:** Space Grotesk, IBM Plex Sans, and JetBrains Mono only. Inter, Roboto, and Arial are explicitly excluded.

### Font Assignments

#### Space Grotesk — Display & Identity
**Role:** H1, H2, product wordmark, hero subheadline, pricing card tier names

Space Grotesk carries a geometric confidence that reads modern-professional without the VC startup sterility of Inter. Its slightly irregular character shapes give Small Portfolio Hub a human quality — it was made by someone, not generated by a design committee.

```
Wordmark:       Space Grotesk 700 (Bold), Trust Blue #2563eb
H1 Hero:        Space Grotesk 700, ~56px / 3.5rem, color: #1e293b
H2 Section:     Space Grotesk 600 (SemiBold), ~36px / 2.25rem, color: #2563eb
H3 Card title:  Space Grotesk 600, ~22px / 1.375rem, color: #1e293b
Pricing tiers:  Space Grotesk 700, ~20px / 1.25rem
```

#### IBM Plex Sans — Body & UI
**Role:** All body copy, navigation links, button labels, form labels, feature descriptions, support text, legal/fine print

IBM Plex Sans was built for clarity in dense information environments — exactly what a property management dashboard demands. Its optical sizing holds up at 13px in a data table and at 18px in a landing page paragraph without feeling like two different products. The "Plex" origin in IBM's documentation systems makes it subtly trustworthy — this is the font of things that have to be accurate.

```
Body large:     IBM Plex Sans 400, 18px / 1.125rem, line-height: 1.7, color: #1e293b
Body regular:   IBM Plex Sans 400, 16px / 1rem, line-height: 1.6
Body small:     IBM Plex Sans 400, 14px / 0.875rem, color: #64748b
Button label:   IBM Plex Sans 600, 15px / 0.9375rem, letter-spacing: 0.01em
Nav link:       IBM Plex Sans 500, 15px
Caption/legal:  IBM Plex Sans 400, 12px / 0.75rem, color: #64748b
```

#### JetBrains Mono — Data & Numbers
**Role:** Rent amounts, transaction figures, pricing numbers, fee comparisons, any tabular financial data, code-like precision callouts

JetBrains Mono applied to financial figures does two things simultaneously: it signals that these numbers were engineered to be exact (trust), and it visually separates financial data from prose so landlords scanning for their number can find it instantly. A landlord looking at "$49/mo — all in" in JetBrains Mono reads differently than the same string in IBM Plex Sans. It reads like a receipt, not a promise.

```
Pricing hero number:  JetBrains Mono 700, 48px / 3rem, color: #1e293b
Pricing /mo label:    JetBrains Mono 400, 20px, color: #64748b
Fee comparison table: JetBrains Mono 500, 15px
Savings callout:      JetBrains Mono 600, Action Orange #f97316
Dashboard figures:    JetBrains Mono 400, 14px
```

### Typographic Scale Summary
```
Display (H1):     Space Grotesk 700 — 56px
Section (H2):     Space Grotesk 600 — 36px
Card head (H3):   Space Grotesk 600 — 22px
Sub-head (H4):    IBM Plex Sans 600 — 18px
Body large:       IBM Plex Sans 400 — 18px
Body:             IBM Plex Sans 400 — 16px
Label/caption:    IBM Plex Sans 400 — 12–14px
Data/price:       JetBrains Mono  — 14–48px (context-dependent)
```

## 4. LANDING PAGE STRUCTURE

> Architecture follows a **Jobs-to-Be-Done narrative arc**: Landlord arrives frustrated → recognizes themselves → understands the alternative → trusts the product → converts. Every section has an assigned conversion role.

---

### SECTION 01 — ABOVE-THE-FOLD HERO
**Conversion role:** Immediate ICP qualification + primary CTA delivery
**SXO requirement:** CTA visible without scroll on all viewports (375px mobile → 1440px desktop)

**Layout:** Two-column on desktop (text left, product screenshot right), single-column stacked on mobile (text above, screenshot below the fold by 20% to imply scroll depth)

**Elements:**
- **Navigation bar** — Trust Blue `#2563eb` background, white wordmark (Space Grotesk 700), ghost nav links (IBM Plex Sans 500 white), single orange CTA button top-right: "Start Free — No Card Needed" (Action Orange `#f97316` fill, white label)
- **H1** — Space Grotesk 700, `#1e293b`:
  *"Property Management Software Built for Landlords with 3–10 Units"*
- **Hero subhead** — IBM Plex Sans 400, 20px, `#64748b`:
  *"One flat monthly price. Live chat support. No hidden EFT fees. Everything Buildium Essential isn't."*
- **Primary CTA button** — Trust Blue fill, white IBM Plex Sans 600 label: "Start Your Free 14-Day Trial"
- **Secondary CTA link** — Action Orange text, no fill: "See how pricing works ↓"
- **Trust bar** (below CTA, inline): Three IBM Plex Sans 400 12px badges in `#64748b` with Trust Blue checkmark icons:
  - ✓ No setup fees
  - ✓ No per-transaction charges
  - ✓ Cancel anytime
- **Hero image:** Dashboard screenshot showing rent collection overview — clean, real UI, not a generic mockup blob

**Mobile adaptation:** H1 drops to 36px / Space Grotesk 700. CTA button is full-width. Trust bar wraps to single column. Screenshot is cropped to dashboard header only (maintains credibility without requiring scroll).

---

### SECTION 02 — PAIN RECOGNITION / COMPETITOR MIRROR
**Conversion role:** "You're describing me exactly" moment — confirms ICP, builds attention
**SXO role:** Surfaces competitor comparison keywords naturally in body copy (Buildium alternative, property management for small landlords, hidden EFT fees)

**Layout:** Dark-mode contrast section — `#1e293b` background, white reversed type. Full-width. This section acts as a visual "chapter break" that resets attention before the product pitch.

**Header** — Space Grotesk 600, white, 36px:
*"Buildium was built for property management companies. Not for you."*

**Body** — IBM Plex Sans 400, 18px, `#cbd5e1` (light on dark):
Three-sentence paragraph. Naming the pain specifically: email-only support, EFT fees per transaction, pricing tiers designed to push landlords into $192+/month plans they don't need.

**Callout card** (inset, `#ffffff` card on dark section background, `#f97316` left border):
*"Buildium Essential: $2.35 per EFT transaction. 50 payments/month = $117 in invisible fees on top of your plan price."*
Numbers rendered in JetBrains Mono 600, Action Orange for the fee amounts.

**Closing line** — IBM Plex Sans 500, white, italic-weight:
*"Small Portfolio Hub charges one number. Every month. On the invoice."*

---

### SECTION 03 — FEATURE GRID / SOLUTION PROOF
**Conversion role:** Feature-to-pain matching — shows the product is complete, not a lite toy
**Layout:** 3-column card grid (desktop), 1-column stack (mobile)

**Section H2** — Space Grotesk 600, Trust Blue `#2563eb`:
*"Everything You Need. Nothing You'll Get Nickel-and-Dimed For."*

**Six feature cards** — White `#ffffff` cards, `#e2e8f0` border, subtle shadow on hover. Each card:
- Trust Blue icon (24px, consistent icon set — Heroicons or Lucide)
- Space Grotesk 600 H3 card title
- IBM Plex Sans 400 body (2–3 lines max)

**Card content:**
1. **Rent Collection** — "ACH/EFT payments included at no extra charge per transaction. Flat fee. Always."
2. **Live Chat Support** — "Real humans, real-time. Not a ticket queue. Not next-business-day email. Now."
3. **Lease Management** — "State-specific templates, e-signatures, and renewal reminders in one place."
4. **Maintenance Tracking** — "Tenant submits. You assign. Both sides get notified. Simple thread, no chaos."
5. **Guided Onboarding** — "Your first unit set up in under 20 minutes with a step-by-step wizard — or live chat if you get stuck."
6. **Transparent Pricing** — "One price per month. See the full invoice breakdown before you're charged. No surprises."

**"Most Popular" badge** (if applicable): Action Orange `#f97316` pill, IBM Plex Sans 600 12px white label.

---

### SECTION 04 — PRICING (THE CONVERSION ENGINE)
**Conversion role:** Close. This section must do the heavy lifting. Every element earns its pixel.
**SXO role:** Price-explicit content improves long-tail search capture ("property management software $49/mo", "buildium alternative flat fee")

**Section H2** — Space Grotesk 600, `#1e293b`:
*"One Plan. One Price. No Math Required."*

**Section subhead** — IBM Plex Sans 400, `#64748b`:
*"We don't have tiers designed to push you into the next one."*

**Pricing card — centered, max-width 480px, elevated shadow:**
- Background: `#ffffff`
- Top border: 4px solid Trust Blue `#2563eb`
- Plan name: Space Grotesk 700, 20px, Trust Blue
- Price: JetBrains Mono 700, 48px, `#1e293b` — **"$49"** with JetBrains Mono 400 20px `#64748b` — **/mo**

- Feature checklist: IBM Plex Sans 400 16px `#1e293b`, Trust Blue checkmark icons, 8 items
